Attendance bot fetcher

After you log in to the MSRIT parents portal yourself, this reads your attendance from that session and sends it to your Telegram bot. It never logs in, fills in or clicks anything on the portal: the portal refuses logins done by an extension.

The PUSH_TOKEN set on the bot.

Reads

The bot wants numbers once a day from 18:30 (it reminds you in Telegram) and after /refresh. Log in to the portal in a normal tab, get past the verification step and stay on the page for a few seconds: the extension's icon shows … while it reads and ✓ once it's sent. To send right away, click the icon while you're on a portal page.

Automatic reads are paused after a read failed (). They resume after the next read that works: click the icon on a portal page.
