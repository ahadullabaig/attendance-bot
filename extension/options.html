Attendance bot fetcher

Log in to the MSRIT parents portal yourself, then click this extension's icon. It reads your attendance from that session and sends it to your Telegram bot, which replies with the summary and what changed since your last read. It never logs in, never fills in or clicks anything on the portal, and does nothing until you click: the portal is built to keep automation out.

The PUSH_TOKEN set on the bot.

How to read

Log in to the portal in a normal tab and get past the verification step. On any portal page after that, click the icon and stay on the page for a few seconds: it shows … while it reads and ✓ once the summary is in Telegram. A ! means it didn't work; the reason is below.
